# Story Background Material

Working notes for a **sci-fi / speculative interactive game**.

## One-paragraph pitch
A disgraced psychiatrist, **Dr. Elias Wren**, co-built a device with his fraternal twin brother **Ezra** — Elias the psychiatrist, Ezra the engineer of mechanical and biological systems. Each was half of the apparatus; neither could have built it alone. The device lets a trained operator enter a patient's dream while they sleep in REM. Decades ago, during development, Ezra was the patient and Elias was the operator, and Elias came back from that dream alone. Ezra is alive but devastated, kept somewhere quiet in the house. Everything Elias has built since is a long-running effort to find a way back to him, paid for by helping other people along the way. His apprentice, **Ashley** — once a patient themselves — is the current operator. Patients believe they are receiving hypnotherapy. They are not. The player is Ashley, going in.

## Hard rules (enforce in any future writing)
- **Ashley's sex/gender is never revealed.** Pronouns: they/them. Description must stay ambiguous.
- **The dream IS the therapy.** What Ashley does inside the dream directly changes the patient's waking mind. Every session writes onto the patient.
- **The risk is asymmetric and one-sided. The operator (Ashley) is structurally safe. The patient is not.** Ashley always comes back from a dream intact, in body and mind. Patients can be healed, unhelped, or harmed. Ezra is the precedent — he was the *patient* when the worst case occurred. The operator's only toll is physical exhaustion. This asymmetry is the ethical spine of the story; do not undermine it.
- **Two layers of deception, one nested inside the other.**
  - Patients are told they are receiving hypnotherapy. They are not.
  - **Ashley is told the work is for the patients. It is also for Ezra.** Ashley does not know about Ezra. The inner circle who do are Wren, Mrs. Halloran, and the Nurse.
- The device's existence is a secret kept from patients and the public. Documentation = prison. Secrecy is a precondition for the work, not a preference.

## Established (load-bearing facts)
- Format: Game / interactive
- Genre: Sci-fi / speculative, weird-surreal tone
- Setting: Present day. The **Wren family home** — the house Elias and Ezra grew up in, with grounds, near an unspecified city. Elias did not buy a clinic; he transformed his childhood home into one.
- Player character: Ashley (always seen as themselves inside dreams)
- Hub character: Wren (operates / monitors from outside, two-way voice comms with Ashley)
- Caseload: up to 6 in-patients in residence at a time, weekly cycle, multiple sessions per patient
- The device has three wired-in positions — **patient, operator, observer (passive)** — plus an **external monitor** at the rig's instruments.
- **At game-start, Ashley has never been the operator on a patient.** They have been the monitor and (via the third chair) a passive observer in Elias's sessions for the last year. The opening of the game is their first real operator's session — the tutorial.
- A **secret seventh resident** — Ezra, who lives in what was once their parents' suite (bedroom, parlour, study, bathroom, conservatory), closed to Ashley
- **Ezra's damage** — his mind is shattered: present, memory, and imagination overlaid without partition. Hallucinates, hears voices, drifts. Self-sufficient in basics, never coherent for long. He is, in effect, the device's signature dream-state running permanently in a waking mind.
- **Inner circle of three** — Wren, Mrs. Halloran, the Nurse — all know about Ezra and the device. Ashley does not.
- The wider property is a **working commercial farm**. The Carter family — **Alison** (the Nurse), **David** (the Groundskeeper, her husband), and **Ryan** (their fifteen-year-old son, at boarding school most of the year) — live in the **converted stables**.
- **Working timeline:** Ezra's accident was about twenty years ago. The Carters were hired then, in their early twenties; they are now early forties. After the accident, Elias spent years developing safeguards and animal-testing the rebuilt device, culminating in chimpanzee work, before resuming human trials in a private sanitorium for mild cases. Edith was an early sanitorium patient who stayed to help. The current residential clinic is a later expansion. Ashley arrived a year ago.
- The device was co-built by **Elias and his fraternal twin brother Ezra**: Elias contributed the psychiatry, Ezra contributed the engineering. Neither could have built it alone.
- The brothers' lifelong pet names for each other are **"Ez" and "El."** Elias is now the only person alive who uses either name.
- The brothers were **academic researchers** pushing a heretical theory — that psychological injury can be treated by direct technical intervention. The field met them with scepticism. Frustration at not being believed drove them to take risks.
- **Ezra's injury was the result of one such risk-taking session**, with Ezra as patient and Elias as operator. The injury forced medical treatment, which forced public record, which cost Elias his medical license and discredited the brothers' research. **The establishment never believed the device worked, so they never confiscated it. Elias still has it.**
- Ezra is alive but mentally devastated, kept in the parents' suite of the house. Everything Elias has built since is in service of finding a way back to him.
- Patients heal, and they also serve as test benches for what Wren may one day try at home — Ashley does not yet fully see this.
- Ezra understood the engineering better than Elias does; some of how the device works is locked inside the mind it broke. The original device is irreplaceable.
- Wren broke the safety rule (no monitor) for Ashley's own treatment — the foundation of their bond.

## Major open threads
- **The first patient case (the tutorial).** Who they are, what they're suffering from, what their dream looks like, what the intervention is.
- **The mid-game session in which Ashley operates on Elias.** What is Elias suffering from? What does Ashley find inside that begins exposing Ezra?
- **The cohort of six (and the wider rotation across the game).** The patient cases that fill the standard loop between Ezra sessions.
- **The number of Ezra sessions** and what each one recovers / reveals.
- **The endgame.** When Ezra wakes, what happens to the household, the practice, the deception, Wren himself, Ashley?
- **Wren's personal life pre-clinic** — beyond Ezra. Marriage? Children? Surviving family?
- **The clinic's money** — what pays for the house, the staff, the device, and Ezra's long quiet care? (Probably the farm, but worth confirming.)
- **The exit phrase** — small but specific texture detail.
