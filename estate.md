# The Estate

## What it is
The Wren family property. A large home with grounds, **the house Elias and Ezra grew up in**, near an unspecified city. Inherited and now wholly owned and operated by Elias.

The estate has two faces:

- **The clinic**, run discreetly out of the main house — hypnotherapy as far as the world is concerned (see [clinic.md](clinic.md)).
- **A commercial farm**, run actively across the grounds — the public, visible work of the property.

The farm is functional, not decorative. It produces, it employs, it has the rhythms of a working agricultural year. This matters in two ways. First, **it is the visible source of income** — the property is plausibly self-sustaining, and explains how Elias funds the household, the staff, and the long quiet care of his brother without raising questions. Second, **it is cover.** The estate is a known agricultural operation in the region; visitors, deliveries, and the comings-and-goings of farm life all give the place a public reason to exist that has nothing to do with the work upstairs.

## Buildings on the property

- **The main house.** The clinic, the device, and most of the family-of-survivors. Elias, Edith, and Ashley live here; Ezra lives in the closed parents' suite (see [collaborator.md](collaborator.md)).
- **The converted stables.** The original Victorian stable block, refurbished into a private residence for the Nurse, her husband (the Groundskeeper), and their son when he is home. They have their own front door, their own life, their own evenings. They are on the property without being inside it.
- **Farm buildings.** Whatever the operation requires — sheds, barns, machinery housing, hands' quarters during seasonal work. (Specifics TBD; depends on what the farm grows or raises.)
- **Possible legacy outbuildings from the animal-testing phase.** Elias spent years (see [history.md](history.md)) testing the device on animals before resuming human trials, culminating in chimpanzee work. The animals were kept somewhere on the property. Whatever housed them — converted barn, purpose-built lab outbuilding, repurposed stable section — likely still exists in some form. David, as groundskeeper, would have managed the practical side of the housing without necessarily knowing the purpose. Worth deciding whether any of that infrastructure remains visible on the estate, and whether the player ever encounters it.

## People on the property

Inside the main house, beyond the patient cohort:
- **Elias Wren** — owner, lives on site ([protagonist.md](protagonist.md))
- **Ezra Wren** — closed parents' suite ([collaborator.md](collaborator.md))
- **Mrs. Edith Halloran** — long-time housekeeper, lives in ([clinic.md](clinic.md))
- **Ashley** — apprentice, has a room in the house ([apprentice.md](apprentice.md))

In the converted stables:
- **Alison Carter** — the Nurse: primary carer for Ezra, secondary house matron for in-patients ([nurse.md](nurse.md))
- **David Carter** — the Groundskeeper, Alison's husband; manages the estate and the commercial farm
- **Ryan Carter** — their son, fifteen, away at boarding school most of the year, home during holidays

The Carters were **hired as a pair in their early twenties**, immediately after Ezra's accident, to care for him and run the property. They have been on the estate for around twenty years; their entire adult lives have been bound up with this household. They met here, married here, raised Ryan here. (See [nurse.md](nurse.md), [history.md](history.md).)
